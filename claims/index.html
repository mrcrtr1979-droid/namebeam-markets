What our own record can test

NameBeam holds daily AI answers from four engines in 27 markets since 2026-08-02. For the rows below, this is the test we plan to run. No results have been computed, so none appear here. Rows with no test listed need data we do not hold, such as visitor conversions or survey responses.

  • Row 5 and row 12, invisible businesses. For each market and category, take the businesses in the Google top 10 results. Count the share never named by any AI engine across all days since 2026-08-02. Report API answers and the logged-out ChatGPT app separately. Row 12 uses the same test across all business types in our 27 markets.
  • Rows 7 and 9, Reddit share of Perplexity citations. Measure the share of Perplexity API citations that are reddit.com, per day and per market. Local buyer questions may differ from the product categories in the published figures.
  • Row 8, Reddit in ChatGPT answers. Two measures: the share of OpenAI answers containing any reddit.com citation, and reddit.com's share of all citations. Plot both daily to see whether swings like Semrush's 60% to 10% appear.
  • Row 14, run-to-run noise. For a fixed business and question, compute the standard deviation of its daily mention rate over 60 days. A claimed lift such as 40% to 80% can then be compared with that noise. We will also test whether visibility percentage over repeated runs is stable, using a split-half correlation.
  • Row 21, rank consistency. Three measures, run per engine and separately for API and app: the share of days the same business is named twice in a row for the same market and question; the share of consecutive-day pairs with an identical ordered list; the share of days a business holds position 1.
  • Row 22, AI Overviews. Our Google results capture can count the share of local buyer questions that show an AI Overview. It holds no click data, so it can test prevalence only, not click loss.

Method

Date checked: 2026-10-05. For each claim we read the page where it was published, followed its citation to the primary source, and compared the two. Quotes are copied from the pages as read on that date. Social posts and forum threads were not read in full and are excluded.

SOURCED
The number exists at the primary source and says what is claimed. We did not audit the primary source's own method.
DISTORTED
The number exists at the primary source, but the claim changes its scope, base, unit or meaning.
CIRCULAR
The sources cited restate each other, and the figure traces back to a single origin.
UNSOURCED
We found no primary source for the claim, or the source named does not contain it.
CONTRADICTED
A dated, measured source reports something different from the claim as commonly stated.

A grade describes the source chain, not the business making the claim.
