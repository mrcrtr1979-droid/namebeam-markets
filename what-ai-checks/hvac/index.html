The percent is the share of all saved HVAC answers in this set that matched the row.

The table tells you how often certain words appeared. It cannot tell you whether a statement is true or whether a topic is useful when you hire someone. For example, the word "licensed" counts even if the answer says it could not confirm a license. The estimate row can include the word "quote" on its own, with no mention of a written document. One answer can match several rows. The engines can use different wording, and the pooled numbers do not show which engine used a word. The answers are not advice.
