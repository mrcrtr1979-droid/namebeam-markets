September 2026 AI Visibility Corpus

28 days. 22 US markets. 4 AI assistants + Google SERP. Which businesses the machines named, counted, dated, and published.

What was measured

Every day in September 2026, NameBeam asked the same buyer-intent questions (“best [service] in [city]”-style local queries) to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, with Google SERP as a baseline, across 22 US markets. Every answer was saved with a date. The metric: distinct local businesses each engine named per market, summed across the 28 run days.

Normalized per niche-market, raw totals mislead because markets track different niche counts (Pleasanton 8, Los Angeles 1). We published the correction against our own first headline: see the recap.

Claude is the outlier. It named zero businesses in 15 of 22 markets. In Dallas it named 0 while Gemini named 66 and Perplexity named 41.
The engines disagree market to market. Gemini named 314 distinct businesses in Pleasanton, CA, and 0 in Los Angeles and Maryland. Same questions, same engines, different city, different answer set.
OpenAI is the most consistent namer across all 22 markets, from 262 on nationwide queries down to 6 in Tyler, TX.

Methodology

Corpus: September 2026, 28 run days, 22 US markets (21 metros + 1 nationwide query set).

Systems tested: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Perplexity, Gemini (Google), plus Google SERP as a non-AI baseline.

Metric: distinct local businesses named per engine per market, summed across run days. Answers change over time; no rankings are guaranteed.

Transparency: per-market reports are public and dated. NameBeam tests on itself first, in public, at proof.namebeam.ai.
