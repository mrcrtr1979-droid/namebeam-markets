Palm Beach Tan Is the Most-Named Tanning Brand in Our September Corpus: 88 Namings in 28 Days

October 1, 2026

When AI engines answer "best tanning near me"-style buyer questions, one tanning brand comes up more than any other in our data: Palm Beach Tan. NameBeam's September 2026 corpus recorded 88 namings of the brand across three US markets (62 by Perplexity, 26 in Google results), the highest total of any tanning brand tracked.

Total namings · 28 days · 3 markets

The count breaks down as 62 Perplexity namings and 26 Google citations across Charlotte (NC), Columbus (OH), and Tyler (TX), measured September 1 to 28, 2026. The second most-named tanning brand, Glo Tanning, totaled 48 across Columbus and Tyler.

Methodology

Corpus: September 2026, 28 run days, 22 local US markets. The same buyer-intent questions ("best [service] in [city]"-style local queries) were asked daily to ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini, with Google SERP as a baseline.

Metric: days per market on which each engine named the business, summed across markets. Every market report is public and dated.
