There are two phone books now.

AI assistants and Google name different local businesses. We counted for 28 days across 22 local US markets. 84% of named businesses appear in only one universe.

AS ABOVE, SO BELOW. 3 6 9.
84%

live in a single universe

Of 272 named businesses in the 22 local markets (Perplexity's per-market top ten counted from its saved answer text, and Google's per-market most-cited list from the published reports, non-business phrases removed), 228 (83.8%) appear in only one of the two lists and 44 appear in both (counted from the saved answer text, Sept 1 to 28, 2026). Some of the most AI-dominant businesses in the corpus sit outside Google's top-10 entirely: Arrington Roofing in Dallas, named by Perplexity on 24 days, never appears in Google's most-cited list for Dallas. Others live in both books: Google also cited the sites of All Star Movers (Pleasanton, 10 days), Reliable Heating & Air (Atlanta, 10 days) and Gates Enterprises (Denver, its site gatesroof.com, 6 days).

Perplexity's side of this comparison is counted from the saved answer text (full business name, whole words, spelling variants merged; ties at tenth place broken by name). Google's side is read from the published Most cited tables, because Google results are not answer text, and a business that Google lists under a shorter name counts as unmatched (counted from the saved answer text, Sept 1 to 28, 2026).

So what: every SEO agency on earth is still optimizing for one phone book. The second phone book has different winners, and nobody is reading it. AI answers are 3 to 5 names, not 10 blue links, second place in AI is tenth place in Google.

Method note: the comparison uses published top-10 lists per market with case-insensitive name normalization, and even that understates the mess. Perplexity's own Denver top-10 lists both "Cenco Roofing" and "CENCO Roofing" (same business, counted twice), while Google sometimes cites domains (e.g. allstarmoverscal.com) rather than business names. Normalization debt lives inside our own lists too. The directional finding, two largely disjoint winner sets, is robust to that. Methodology

Runs on September 1 to 28, 2026. Not every engine answered with names every day: Perplexity named businesses on 25 September dates, OpenAI on 28, Gemini on 26 and Anthropic on 28 (text rule, Sept 1 to 28). 22 local US markets, 33 niches. Four AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) plus Google SERP as baseline. Same buyer-intent questions per market, every answer dated. Counts are distinct businesses named per engine per market from the published per-market JSONs; per-business Perplexity day counts from the saved answer text and Google day counts from the published top-10 tables. Engine coverage figures are distinct-businesses-per-market from engine_coverage. Turnover figures compare the first and second half of the month in the saved answer text. Everything here is computed from the September corpus; the 24 per-market reports are public and every figure above can be checked against them. Headline figures are rounded, and the unrounded values are printed alongside.
