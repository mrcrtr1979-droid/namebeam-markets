Directories and AI answers

Expertise.com was the most-cited domain in Perplexity answers, on 250 of 670 cell-days (37.3%), September 1 to 27, 2026. We then compared businesses that appear on the Expertise.com pages Perplexity cited with the other businesses Perplexity named.

  • Across 15 cells that had a cited Expertise.com page, 414 businesses were listed on those pages.
  • Only 57 of those 414 listed businesses (13.8%) were named by Perplexity on at least one day in September.
  • Those 57 businesses were named on a median of 5 days, against about 1 day for other named businesses (each out of 23 to 24 usable answer-days per cell).
This is an association, not proof that a listing causes naming. Listed firms tend to be established and well reviewed, which can drive naming on its own. The Expertise.com lists were read on 2026-10-05, not in September, so membership may differ from what Perplexity saw during the window.

Method and limits

  • Window: September 1 to 27, 2026. Perplexity's collection method changed on 2026-09-28, so this window stops at 09-27.
  • Sources: Perplexity's cited sources and Google's organic top results, reduced to the registrable domain. A domain counts once per cell-day however many URLs cite it.
  • Only research segment rows with a successful run are used. Rows that cite the target business itself were left out.
  • Google results rows with extraction failures (a consent wall returned no data) are excluded: 791 of 1,269 Google rows in the window.
  • Domain types are rule-based, using domain lists and keyword rules with documented manual overrides. They were not hand-reviewed for every domain.
  • Pleasanton, CA accounts for a large share of the underlying rows, and the record is one run per cell per day.
  • The Expertise.com comparison relies on name matching with a similarity threshold and a few manual overrides, and on pages read live on 2026-10-05.
