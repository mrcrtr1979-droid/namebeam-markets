Who AI named: personal injury attorneys in four markets and residential roofing in three

Sample cut from NameBeam Edition 1. Fixed local questions asked once a day through each engine API (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity). Engines are never pooled with each other. A panel is one market plus one niche. An answered day is a day on which one engine returned a non-empty name list for one panel; it counts as naming a business when at least one name survives the Edition 1 name gate. Counts use the Edition 1 name gate and the N.gated.day metric exactly as published (README, metrics/metrics_v2_methods.csv); a spot audit found 86 of 100 gated names to be operating businesses.

Coverage

  • WA, 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-27: Perplexity ran on Sonar chat completions with web search.
  • WB, 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-13: Perplexity ran on the Agent API with a forced web search tool. Perplexity retired Sonar chat completions on 2026-09-27, so WA and WB are reported apart and never pooled; any change between them mixes the method change with change over time. WB is a short window: read its values with their denominators.
  • Totals over the 7 panels in this sample (sum of the panel counts; engines kept apart):
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI API): named at least one business on 49 of 143 answered days (WA Sep 1 to 27)
  • Claude (Anthropic API): named at least one business on 0 of 189 answered days (WA Sep 1 to 27)
  • Gemini (Google API): named at least one business on 88 of 90 answered days (WA Sep 1 to 27)
  • Perplexity (API): named at least one business on 168 of 168 answered days (WA Sep 1 to 27)
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI API): named at least one business on 33 of 99 answered days (WB Sep 28 to Oct 13)
  • Claude (Anthropic API): named at least one business on 0 of 105 answered days (WB Sep 28 to Oct 13)
  • Gemini (Google API): named at least one business on 0 of 0 answered days (WB Sep 28 to Oct 13); no answered days in this window
  • Perplexity (API): named at least one business on 94 of 105 answered days (WB Sep 28 to Oct 13)
  • OpenAI name lists are empty on every row from 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-05 (235 of 235 rows) because the name extractor returned none, so those days are not answered days and OpenAI name counts start 2026-09-06 (README, Known gaps). Gemini answered only part of the questions each day to 2026-09-26 and every Gemini call failed from 2026-09-27, so Gemini has no WB figures (README, Limits). The name lists under-count the businesses an answer mentions, most for Claude (README DQ-5), so a low rate describes the published name lists, not everything an answer said.
