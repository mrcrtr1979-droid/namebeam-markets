How often three AI engines share the same local business name

Study period: 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-09

Finding

Any two of three AI engines share at least one business name 55.8% of the time (95 percent interval 53.6 to 58.1 percent): 1070 of 1916 engine pairs across 788 question-days, 55 questions, 17 run days, 2026-10-24 to 2026-11-09, ChatGPT (OpenAI) gpt-5-mini, Claude (Anthropic) bedrock haiku 4.5, Gemini (Google) flash lite.

Method

Names were extracted mechanically by capitalisation from the public corpus (github.com/mrcrtr1979-droid/namebeam-visibility-index), matched on canonical form. Generic names filtered. Three engines only (ChatGPT gpt-5-mini, Claude bedrock haiku 4.5, Gemini flash lite). Local-business questions only. This is a count of agreement, not a measurement of causes or outcomes.
